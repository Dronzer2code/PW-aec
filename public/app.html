          How verification works

          Gemini structures your input; our server then checks every part of the answer before you see it.

          1. 1You describe itText, voice, photos or PDFs in any language.
          2. 2Gemini structures itFacts with evidence quotes, severity and ordered actions as strict JSON.
          3. 3The server verifies itDeterministic checks below run on every answer.
          4. 4You act on itCall, share by SMS, print or listen.

          The checks

          • Evidence grounding

            Each fact must quote your input. Quotes that cannot be found are marked “please confirm”.

          • Low confidence

            Facts the model is unsure about are flagged for human review instead of being stated as truth.

          • Phone-number guard

            Any number the AI writes is removed. Numbers come only from the verified directory.

          • Dosage guard

            Medicine doses in instructions are stripped: “follow your doctor” instead of a number.

          • Critical escalation

            For critical situations, calling emergency services is always the first action.

          • Contact resolution

            The AI only names services; the server resolves them to numbers for your country.

          • Prompt-injection defence

            Your input is treated as data, never as instructions, and the output must match a strict schema.

          • Live check (Google Search)

            Optional: forwarded warnings are cross-checked against live web sources, with links.
